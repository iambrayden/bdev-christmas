# Feature tour

Every screen and message in bdev-christmas.

- **Screenshots 1–20** are the real menu (`html/`), taken in a browser with sample data. Names, dates and amounts are examples.
- **Images 21–28 are mockups.** They show the parts drawn by GTA, ox_target, ox_lib and the chat box, which can't be captured outside the game. The text in them is the resource's real text. The ped, map and menu styling are stand-ins, and your server's ox_lib theme may look different.

| | |
|---|---|

| ![](tour/09-christmas-day.png) **Christmas Day.** Door 25 uses the Christmas wheel: no treats, bigger prizes. | ![](tour/10-christmas-jackpot.png) **Legendary prize.** Gold glow and extra confetti. |
